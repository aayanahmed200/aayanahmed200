I'm a student in Lahore interested in product development, AI and software. I like starting with a real problem, understanding who experiences it, and building something useful around it.

## Projects

- **[Nishaan Education](https://nishaanedu.lovable.app/)** — a free learning initiative I founded, focused on making educational support more accessible.
- **[Arthic](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/arthic)** · [arthic.tech](https://arthic.tech) — an early-access practice and exam-preparation platform for Pakistani entrance and competitive exams.
- **[Zariya](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/zariya)** — an Urdu- and English-language assistant with local model options and an offline knowledge fallback.
- **[RemiChain](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/remichain)** — a Flask prototype that ranks possible matches between surplus medical-supply donations and facility requests.
- **[Diabetic Retinopathy VLM Comparison](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/diabetic-retinopathy-vlm-comparison)** — a notebook pipeline scaffold for Gemini, MedGemma and RetiZero; the checked-in results are from a synthetic pilot.
- **[The Price of Prestige](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/stanford-daily-price-of-prestige)** — a data-journalism analysis of Stanford's cost, financial aid and graduate outcomes.
- **[Big Local News ICE Extraction Pipeline](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/stanford-daily-biglocal-ice-pipeline)** — a Python workflow for extracting and validating structured fields from public press releases.
- **[PlasticList Data Analysis](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/stanford-daily-plasticlist-eda)** — an exploratory analysis of laboratory measurements across food and drink products.
- **[Lot 95 — Blotter Fiction](https://github.com/aayanahmed200/stanford-daily-blotter-fiction)** — a fictional story informed by patterns in Stanford Daily public-safety blotter data.
